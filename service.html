Manga

Berserk (Japanese: ベルセルク, Hepburn: Beruseruku) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Kentaro Miura. Set in a medieval Europe–inspired dark fantasy world, the story centers on the characters of Guts, a lone swordsman, and Griffith, the leader of a mercenary band called the "Band of the Hawk". The series follows Guts' journey seeking revenge on Griffith, who betrayed him and sacrificed his comrades to become a powerful demonic being.

Anime

Berserk (Japanese: ベルセルク, Hepburn: Beruseruku) is a 2016 Japanese anime television series based on Kentaro Miura's manga series of the same name and an acting sequel to the Golden Age Arc film trilogy. This is the second television series adaptation of the manga after the 1997 anime of the same name, covering the Conviction arc from the manga. A second season, covering the first half of the Hawk of the Millennium Empire arc, aired in 2017.

Merchandise

Berserk merchandise is a tribute to the dark and brutal world of Kentaro Miura’s legendary manga, Berserk. Fans can find a wide variety of merchandise featuring iconic symbols like the Brand S of Sacrifice, Dragon Slayer, and Guts' Berserker Armor, all capturing the series' gritty aesthetic.
